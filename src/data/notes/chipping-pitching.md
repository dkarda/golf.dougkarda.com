Simplify the wedge swing, minimalize the motion, improve the consistency.

- Stand tall, square to the target and comfortably close to the ball.
- Keep the club shaft straight, perpendicular to the shot path.
- Flare out the front foot and put extra weight on the front leg.
- Bring the club back slow, in control.
- Let the weight of the arms and club start the downswing for a smooth, pendulum-like swing.

Enjoy the results and go grab that putter.
