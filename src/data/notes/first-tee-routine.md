title: First-tee routine
# date: 2026-08-12
tags: [on-course, routine]
source: on-course
---

The first tee is where the round gets rushed. Keep it boring. Breathe.

- One rehearsal swing at the very least, to get loose
- Look at the start line, not the trouble

If the driver isn't trusted that morning, don't hesitate to switch over to the 3 or 5-wood.
