date: 2026-08-12

The first tee is where the round gets rushed. Keep it boring.

- Two rehearsal swings with the shot already chosen
- Look at the start line, not the trouble
- Accept the first miss and play the next shot

If the driver isn’t trusted that morning, 3-wood is an automatic — no debate on the box.
