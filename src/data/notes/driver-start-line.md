Worked on starting the ball on a line instead of steering with the hands.

- Alignment stick just outside the ball as a start-line gate
- Feel the clubhead passing the hands after low point, not flipping at it
- If the ball starts right, check face at address before changing the swing

Next range session: ten balls with the gate, ten without, then compare starts.
